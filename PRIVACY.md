# Privacy

SpreadSignal is designed without accounts, sign-in, analytics, or a first-party server.

The app stores no personal profile, health information, location history, or user-submitted plan content on a SpreadSignal service. Preparedness-plan fields remain in the browser until a visitor exports them; exported files are created locally by the visitor’s browser.

When live surveillance data is requested, the visitor’s browser contacts the public CDC data service directly. That service may collect information according to its own policies.

If this deployment adds analytics, advertising, accounts, form submission, or a server, this notice must be updated before launch.
