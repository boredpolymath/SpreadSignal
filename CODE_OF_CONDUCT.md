# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in SpreadSignal to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to help create an environment where people can ask questions, share ideas, and collaborate in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and professional.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and outcomes—not people.
- Welcome different perspectives and lived experiences.
- Assume good intent while making room for clarification and correction.
- Protect sensitive health, security, and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Do not use this project to share identifiable health information or make unsubstantiated public-health claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when conduct conflicts with this policy. To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub and include enough context for a fair review.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the SpreadSignal community in public.
